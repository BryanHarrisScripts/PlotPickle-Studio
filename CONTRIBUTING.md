# Contributing to PlotPickle Studio

PlotPickle is an independent creative project exploring how humans and AI can tell stories together. Ideas, bug reports, focused improvements and independent adaptations are welcome.

Use GitHub Issues to describe problems or proposals, and pull requests for focused improvements. Explain what you changed and how you tested it. AI-assisted development is welcome; contributors remain responsible for understanding and verifying their work.

Respect creative ownership, privacy and human approval. Never include private stories, credentials or personal media, or trigger paid services without authorization. Contributions must respect applicable software and content licences; only submit material you have the right to contribute.

PlotPickle was developed for ChatGPT. Other platform adaptations are welcome, but are independently developed and have not been tested by this project. This is a personal creative project, with no guaranteed support or commitment to merge contributions.
