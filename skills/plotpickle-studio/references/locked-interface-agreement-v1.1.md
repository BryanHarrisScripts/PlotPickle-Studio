## Normative five-choice HOME sitemap

**Home and Studio are aliases to the SAME interactive in-conversation application with Elara.** There is no second mode, app or mandatory panel. Five top-level selectable cards in exact sitemap order:

1. Meet Elara
2. Learn
3. Storyboard
4. Library / Your Stories (the private saved Studio catalogue)
5. Connected Services

The fourth entry was promoted from the old quiet Library placement to a real HOME navigation option. `studio_list_projects` supplies saved private Studio projects plus reference-only sources, labeled distinctly; it is NOT direct browsing of all ChatGPT Library folders. Storyboard's REEL and ONCE remain nested; no REEL/ONCE sixth HOME choice. Secondary Library link and verified Continue Creating are contextual, not additional top-level options.
